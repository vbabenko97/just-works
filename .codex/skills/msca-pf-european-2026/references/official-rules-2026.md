# MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships 2026: European Fellowship core rules

Use this as an offline baseline. Recheck current official sources on the web before making a high-stakes claim.

## Call

- Call: HORIZON-MSCA-2026-PF-01.
- Topic for European Fellowships: HORIZON-MSCA-2026-PF-01-01.
- 2026 call opened 9 April 2026.
- Submission deadline: 9 September 2026, 17:00 CEST.
- Overall indicative PF call budget: EUR 399.05 million; the Work Programme allocates EUR 339.19 million to European Fellowships and EUR 59.86 million to Global Fellowships.

## European Fellowship eligibility baseline

Check the 2026 Work Programme and call page for the controlling wording.

- The researcher may be of any nationality.
- By the call deadline, the researcher must hold a PhD or have successfully defended the doctoral thesis unconditionally even if the formal degree has not yet been awarded.
- Research experience after the PhD award is normally limited to a maximum of 8 years full-time equivalent, subject to the specific exclusions/extensions in the Work Programme and the dedicated 8-year guidance.
- Mobility rule for a European Fellowship: the researcher must not have resided or carried out their main activity in the country of the beneficiary for more than 12 months in the 36 months immediately before the call deadline, subject to the specific Work Programme exceptions.
- European Fellowships last 12-24 months.
- A non-academic placement of up to 6 months at the end of the fellowship may be requested if justified and compliant with the call conditions.
- A proposal uses one beneficiary, an independent legal entity established in an EU Member State or Horizon Europe Associated Country, which employs and supervises the researcher.
- Only one proposal per individual researcher is eligible; where more than one is submitted, the last submitted eligible proposal controls under the Work Programme rule.
- 2026 introduces a resubmission restriction: a proposal involving the same recruiting organisation and individual researcher that scored below 80% under the previous PF call must not be resubmitted the following year. Verify exact scope against the current Work Programme before applying it.

## Evaluation

- Three criteria: Excellence, Impact, Quality and Efficiency of the Implementation.
- Weighting: Excellence 50%, Impact 30%, Implementation 20%.
- Each criterion is scored 0-5 with one decimal place.
- The Work Programme sets an individual threshold of 3/5 for each criterion and an overall threshold of 70% for consideration, subject to the call text.
- Evaluators assess the proposal as submitted. Missing or weak material is not credited merely because it could be fixed later.
- REA states MSCA proposals are evaluated by at least 3 independent external experts.

## Part B mechanics

From the 2026 PF application form/template V5.0 dated 27 March 2026:

- Part B-1 contains Sections 1-3 and has a maximum of 10 pages.
- Excess pages after the deadline are not considered; non-compliant formatting may be reformatted and excess pages disregarded.
- Do not use external hyperlinks in Part B-1 to expand the proposal; evaluators disregard them.
- Part B-2 has no overall page limit.
- CV of the researcher has an indicative length of 5 pages.
- Part A and B must be fully consistent; use full consecutive dates in dd/mm/yyyy format where the template requires them.
- Table 5.2 capacity: maximum 1 page for the beneficiary and maximum 1/2 page for each associated partner.
- Declare relevant inter-relationships between participating organisations/individuals and other entities/persons appearing in the proposal, as required by the template.
- Provide the administrative/legal relation between the department carrying out the work and the PIC-linked legal entity.
- Section 6 additional ethics: only if additional information could not fit in Part A.
- Section 7 additional security: follow the template's conditional instruction.
- Section 8 covers environmental considerations under the MSCA Green Charter.
- Section 9 letter of commitment is required for Global Fellowships' outgoing host, not ordinary European Fellowships.
- Additional B-2 information that should have been in B-1, or is not requested, may be disregarded.

## Generative AI in proposal preparation

The 2026 template includes guidance requiring applicants to remain fully responsible for the proposal, transparently disclose which generative-AI tools were used and how, verify accuracy/validity/appropriateness and citations, check sources and plagiarism risks, and acknowledge AI limitations such as bias, errors, and knowledge gaps. Verify the current template/Guide for Applicants before final submission.

## Evaluation reality

Meeting formal thresholds is not the same as being competitive for funding. Review against the 0-5 evaluator descriptors and aim to eliminate concrete shortcomings, especially under Excellence because of its 50% weight and its role in tie-breaking under the Work Programme.
