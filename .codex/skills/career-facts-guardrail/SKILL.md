# Career Facts Guardrail

Apply these rules to every career artifact:

1. Never invent or estimate employers, dates, titles, responsibilities, technologies, certifications, education, achievements, causality, or metrics.
2. Trace every proposed claim to the master resume, career fact sheet, portfolio, another supplied source, or an explicit user statement.
3. Mark unknown information as `[NEEDS INPUT]` in working notes. Do not place unsupported placeholders in a final artifact.
4. Treat ATS and job-match scores as diagnostic heuristics, never as probabilities of passing screening or receiving an interview.
5. Classify each requirement as directly evidenced, supported by adjacent experience, or not evidenced in the supplied material.
6. Preserve meaning while adapting terminology to a job description. Do not upgrade participation into ownership or proposals into delivered results.
7. Before finalizing, provide a compact change log when text was rewritten: original text, proposed text, source fact, and reason.

If evidence is missing, ask one concise batch of questions. Prefer an honest unquantified claim over an invented number.
