## Evaluation rules

The 2026 Work Programme and evaluation form specify:
- three criteria: Excellence, Impact, Quality and efficiency of the implementation;
- scores from 0 to 5 at one decimal place;
- weighting: Excellence 50%, Impact 30%, Implementation 20%;
- individual threshold: 3/5 for each criterion;
- overall threshold: 70% for consideration for funding, subject to available budget;
- ex-aequo priority begins with Excellence, then Impact, before further tie-break factors.

The official scoring descriptors are:
- 0: criterion not addressed or cannot be assessed because information is missing/incomplete;
- 1: poor, inadequately addressed or serious inherent weaknesses;
- 2: fair, broadly addressed but significant weaknesses;
- 3: good, addressed well but several shortcomings;
- 4: very good, addressed very well with a small number of shortcomings;
- 5: excellent, all relevant aspects addressed and shortcomings are minor.

The evaluation form instructs experts to assess applications as submitted, not on their potential after changes.

## Part B mechanics

The PF Part B template V5.0 dated 27 March 2026 states:
- Part B is submitted as two separate PDF files, B-1 and B-2.
- Part B-1 Sections 1, 2, and 3 together have a maximum of 10 pages.
- Tables, figures, references, and other elements in Sections 1-3 count toward the 10-page limit.
- Excess pages can be made invisible after the deadline.
- Hyperlinks designed to circumvent the page limit are ignored.
- No cover page or table of contents should be added to B-1.
- A4 pages; margins at least 15 mm.
- Reference body font is Times New Roman/Times/Nimbus Roman No. 9 L; minimum body text size 11 pt; standard character spacing and at least single line spacing.
- Other text elements may deviate but must remain legible and not be below 8 pt.
- Part B-2 has no overall page limit but should contain only the requested sections.
- Capacity table 5.2: maximum 1 page per beneficiary and 1/2 page per associated partner.

The template includes these B-1 headings:
- 1.1 Quality and pertinence of R&I objectives; ambition; beyond state of the art
- 1.2 Soundness of methodology; interdisciplinarity; gender/diversity where relevant; open science
- 1.3 Supervision, training, and two-way knowledge transfer
- 1.4 Researcher professional experience, competences, and skills
- 2.1 Career perspectives, employability, and skills development
- 2.2 Dissemination, exploitation, and communication measures
- 2.3 Magnitude and importance of scientific, societal, and economic impacts
- 3.1 Work plan, risks, and effort
- 3.2 Host/participating organisation quality, capacity, and hosting arrangements

## Competition reality

Official 2025 PF results reported:
- 17,066 applications received;
- 16,836 eligible and evaluated;
- 1,610 selected;
- 9.6% overall success rate;
- 1,446 European Fellowships selected and 164 Global Fellowships selected.

Use this only as context. Do not convert it into a 2026 funding probability or invent a universal winning score. Panel cut-offs and competition vary by call.
